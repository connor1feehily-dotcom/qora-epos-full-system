🧠 Core Functional Modules
Inventory Management

Tracks stock levels in real time.

Supports barcode scanning, batch tracking, expiry dates.

Auto-reorder triggers when stock falls below threshold.

Product & Pricing Management

Add/edit/delete products with categories, SKUs, and variants.

Dynamic pricing rules (e.g. time-based discounts, loyalty pricing).

VAT and tax configuration per product.

Purchase Order Management

Create and send POs to suppliers.

Track order status (pending, shipped, received).

Auto-generate POs based on low stock alerts.

Supplier & Customer Database

Store contact info, payment terms, and order history.

Assign preferred suppliers to products.

Customer profiles for loyalty tracking and targeted promotions.

Sales Ledger & Transaction History

View all POS transactions with timestamps and staff IDs.

Filter by date, product, or customer.

Export to CSV or PDF for audits.

📊 Analytics & Reporting
Real-Time Dashboards

Visual KPIs: daily sales, top products, shrinkage, etc.

Customizable widgets for different roles (manager, buyer, etc.).

Advanced Reporting

Sales by category, time, staff, or location.

Inventory turnover, dead stock, and shrinkage reports.

Profit margin analysis and forecasting.

Audit Trails

Logs every change made in the system (who, what, when).

Useful for compliance and accountability.

🛠️ Operational Tools
Cash Management

Track float, cash drops, and end-of-day reconciliation.

Alert for discrepancies or unusual activity.

Staff Management

Role-based access control (e.g. cashier vs. manager).

Shift scheduling and clock-in/out tracking.

Performance metrics (sales per hour, upsells, etc.).

Promotions & Discounts Engine

Create BOGOF, mix-and-match, or time-limited offers.

Apply discounts by product, category, or customer group.

Schedule promotions in advance.

Returns & Refunds Handling

Process returns with reason codes.

Restock or write-off returned items.

Link refunds to original transactions.

🌐 Integration & Automation
POS Sync

Syncs product/pricing updates to POS terminals in real time.

Pulls sales data back into the back office for analysis.

E-commerce Integration

Sync inventory and orders with online store.

Unified stock control across channels.

Mobile Access

Manage inventory, view reports, or approve POs from a tablet or phone.

Task Automation

Schedule reports, auto-generate POs, or trigger alerts (e.g. low margin warning).

🔐 Security & Compliance
User Permissions & Logs

Fine-grained access control.

Logs of login attempts and data access.

Data Backup & Recovery

Scheduled backups to cloud or local storage.

Restore points in case of failure.

Regulatory Compliance

GDPR-ready data handling.

Tax reporting formats for local authorities.
